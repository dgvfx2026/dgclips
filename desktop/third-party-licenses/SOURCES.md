# Editorial components

- llama.cpp b11936: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/b11936 (MIT). Official Windows Vulkan runtime. LLVM OpenMP notices accompany its archive.
- Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (Apache-2.0). Quantized Q4_K_M by https://huggingface.co/lmstudio-community/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507-GGUF. The quantized weights are downloaded separately by the app and remain unmodified.
- YuNet face detector: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet (MIT, Shiqi Yu).
- Versions, source URLs and SHA-256 are recorded in editorial-assets.json and face-manifest.json.
- Caption fonts and their OFL notices are in fonts/.

- Whisper large-v3-turbo: https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo (MIT). CTranslate2 conversion: https://huggingface.co/dropbox-dash/faster-whisper-large-v3-turbo. Exact files and SHA-256 are recorded in model-manifest.json.
